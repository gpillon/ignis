ignis is deliberately narrow: **one model family, NVIDIA RTX cards**. It
serves Qwen3.8 (the 27B and the 125B Flash-Next MoE) and Qwen-Image-2.1 on a
single card, every model on an RTX 5090 or an RTX PRO 6000, the 27B on a 16 GB
RTX 50 card, or from 0.10.0 an RTX 3090, too
([What it runs on](../start/index.md#what-it-runs-on)), and it gives up
generality to take back speed. Everything in it is shaped around the load a
developer actually produces: **one main agent plus a handful of subagents,
hitting the same card at once**, with long contexts, tool calls and reasoning.

These pages tell what that buys, one topic at a time. Each one says how it
works, what was measured and on what, how you see it and switch it, and where
it stops. Every number links to the finding that measured it.

Measured on one RTX 5090 with 64 GB of DDR4-3200 on a PCIe 3.0 board, the
27B NVFP4 unless a tile says otherwise. [How these numbers are made](how-we-measure.md)
says what each kind of figure is, and what it is not.
