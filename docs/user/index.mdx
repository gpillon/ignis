## What sets it apart

One model family on NVIDIA RTX cards, built for the load an agent makes: a
main conversation and its subagents on the same GPU at once.

**What it runs on.** RTX 5090 and RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell with every model, and
the 16 GB RTX 50 cards with the 16 GB entries. The RTX 3090 and 3090 Ti are
supported, on the 27B's 16 GB entries. The RTX 4090 and the 16 GB RTX 40 cards
are verified for correctness: the release's sm_86 gate passed on an Ada
Lovelace card (sm_89)
([the finding](../findings/2026-11-07-sm86-gate-passes-on-sm89.md)). The releases
are built for compute capabilities 8.6 and 12.0, so RTX 30 and RTX 40 cards run
them. The NVFP4 builds, Flash-Next and the
image model load on Blackwell alone, on a card of 32 GB or more
([the details](start/#what-it-runs-on), and
[every model on every card](reference/models.md#where-each-model-loads)).
The figures below are
measured on an RTX 5090.
