- Summary: Under 16 GiB and 16 GiB less 1 GiB on the 5090 the 27B's trellis artifact adapts nothing (262,144 tokens on two lanes) and serves a cold 131,072-token prompt, less 3 GiB adapts to 65,536, and the plan is within 1% of the corrected estimate.

## Question

A 16 GB card has a desktop on it. With 0, 1 and 3 GiB of the 16 GiB taken, does the 16 GB artifact load, adapt as spec 04
says, and serve a 128K prompt on both lanes? And do the plan's lines match the study's estimates within 2%?

## Evidence

Build `340-gates` (release CUDA server and bench). Every load: the trellis artifact, `--profile rtx50-16gb`, an explicit
`--vram-budget-bytes`, `IDLE_UNLOAD=0`, loop guard at its default; chunk 1,024, hq-e8-2b, 2 lanes, DFlash2 7 / full head,
rope none; the desktop's ~2.5 GB held on the 5090. The owner's decision of 2026-10-24: at 16 GiB less 3 GiB the serving leg
runs at the context the load adapts to, and this finding says that 128K does not fit there and by how much; no
configuration change. Raw logs, plan events, JSON: `.scratch/340/capacity/` (untracked).

### B3's ladder (plan-only refusals, `gpu-tests.log`)

- 262,144: the pool, 2,495 pages, is under the floor of 4,100 (4,096 plus 4 retained): refused.
- 196,608: the floor (3,072 + 4 pages) needs 14,301,026,404 B, over B3 by 342,382,692 B (581 pages): the plan's own floor
  cuts it.
- 131,072: the floor needs 13,697,038,436 B and fits, with 261,605,276 B (443 pages) to spare. One sequence plus the spare
  reservation (3,072 pages) needs 14,298,658,916 B: **short by 340,015,204 B = 577 pages = 36,928 tokens**. The rule that cuts
  it is `vram.adapt_spare_tokens`, 65,536 tokens at 2 lanes (ADR 0063 section 3). With a spare of at most 28,608 tokens
  the ladder would have kept 131,072. Not changed (owner's decision).
- 65,536: one sequence plus the spare needs 13,694,670,948 B and fits: kept; lanes stay 2, because a context fits at 2 lanes.
- A 128K request at B3 (`B3-refuse.jsonl`): 131,072 prompt tokens plus 64 got HTTP 400 `context_length_exceeded`, "request
  needs 131136 tokens (prompt + max_tokens), over the model's 65536-token context". `/v1/models` reports `max_model_len`
  65,536 (`B3-models.json`).

**The corrected estimate.** The workspace is 0.3423 GB of chunk scratch plus 0.0923 GB of DFlash2 append, with **no 4,096

## Finding

Observed:

1. **B0 and B1 load with nothing adapted** at 262,144 on two lanes and serve a cold 131,072-token prompt (B1 measured, B0 by
   #339 A2), coherent, both lanes decoding together (both TTFTs before either done).
2. **B3 adapts to 65,536 on two lanes** and does not serve 128K: the ladder's 131,072 step is 577 pages (340,015,204 B,
   36,928 tokens) short once `vram.adapt_spare_tokens` (65,536 at two lanes) is held back; a 128K request is refused with a
   400. The leg runs at 65,472 prompt tokens, cold, coherent.
3. **The plan equals the corrected formulas within 1% on every line and 0.42% on the pool.** Against the study's formulas
   as written the workspace is 71.4% (at 262,144) and 38.5% (at 65,536) lower, and the KV pool 22.9-42.5% higher, for one
   reason: the study's workspace carried a 4,096 B per token hq prompt plane that ADR 0061 removed, and a context term.

Inferred:

- Of the 3 GiB, the cost to the context is the KV pool alone (5,461 pages): everything else is fixed and independent of the
  context on this body, so a larger context at B3 is bought with a smaller spare reservation, not with any other line.
- With `adapt_spare_tokens` at 28,608 or less B3 would have kept 131,072 (read from the arithmetic of the plan-only
  refusals; not run as a load).

## Implications

- Acceptance 5's "within 2% of the estimates" holds only for the corrected estimates; the study's `vram_plan.py` workspace
  and pool lines are stale for this body and should not be used to size a 16 GB card. A user on a card with 3 GiB of desktop
  gets 65,536 tokens unless `vram.adapt_spare_tokens` is lowered.
- The corrected workspace (0.4346 GB, flat in the context) is the number to size a card with.

## Limits and unknowns

- A 5090 emulates a 16 GB card's memory only: not its bandwidth or SM count (see the speed finding).
- The budget is subtracted whole at load: a real desktop's use moves during a session.
- B1's and B3's legs are one sample each after a warm-up on a soup prompt (no agent traffic); tokens per round there say
  little of agent use.
- The 131,072 shortfall at B3 is read from plan-only refusals, exact for the plan, not from a second full load.
- The B3 leg's 65,472-token prompt follows from max_tokens 64; a longer output budget lowers it.

## Follow-ups

- Whether the spare reservation at two lanes should be lower on a card class with a desktop is a product decision for the
  owner; this finding does not make it.
